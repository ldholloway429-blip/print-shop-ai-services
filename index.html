Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to learn any AI tools?

No. That's the whole point — you never touch the AI. You send a photo or an idea, we deliver the finished result. If you can send a text message, you can use this.

Who approves the work before it goes live?

You do — everything comes to you first for a one-tap approval. Nothing posts, prints, or publishes without your say-so.

What if I don't like a design?

Then we redo it. The Design Retainer includes unlimited revision rounds — we iterate until you're happy, or you don't pay for that piece.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No. Every plan is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. Paid in advance, no hidden fees.

How is this different from hiring a designer?

Speed and price. AI does the heavy lifting in hours instead of weeks, a real human (us) reviews everything, and you pay a flat monthly rate instead of per-project invoices.
